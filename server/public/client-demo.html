🌐 Welcome to My Website

This is a demo of PushSaaS Subscription Bell Integration

✅ PushSaaS Widget is Active!

Look for the bell icon 🔔 in the bottom-right corner of your screen.

How This Works

  • Automatic Integration: The PushSaaS widget loads automatically when you add the meta tags and script
  • Customizable: All appearance settings are controlled from your PushSaaS dashboard
  • User-Friendly: Visitors can subscribe with one click after granting notification permission
  • Privacy-Focused: Users can unsubscribe anytime by clicking the bell

Integration Code

Here's what we added to this page to make the subscription bell work:

<!-- Step 1: Add configuration meta tags --> <meta name="pushsaas-site-id" content="1"> <meta name="pushsaas-server-url" content="http://localhost:3000"> <!-- Step 2: Load the widget script (before closing body tag) --> <script src="http://localhost:3000/pushsaas-widget.js"></script>

📝 For Production

Replace the server URL with your production PushSaaS server:

<meta name="pushsaas-server-url" content="https://api.pushsaas.com"> <script src="https://api.pushsaas.com/pushsaas-widget.js"></script>

Try It Out!

  1. Click the bell icon in the bottom-right corner 🔔
  2. Click "SUSCRIBIRSE" to subscribe
  3. Grant notification permissions when prompted
  4. You'll receive a confirmation notification
  5. Click the bell again to see your subscription status
  6. You can unsubscribe anytime by clicking "DESUSCRIBIRSE"

⚙️ Customize Your Bell

To customize colors, text, position, and behavior:

  1. Visit: http://localhost:3000/subs-bell.html?siteId=1
  2. Login with your admin credentials
  3. Customize all settings
  4. Click "Actualizar Configuración" to save
  5. Refresh this page to see your changes!

Features You Can Customize

  • 🎨 Appearance: Rounded or Square bell, Dark or Light theme, Custom colors
  • 📍 Position: Bottom-Right, Bottom-Left, Top-Right, or Top-Left
  • 📝 Messages: Custom titles and button text for each state
  • 🔔 Recent Notifications: Show/hide last 3 notifications in the modal
  • 👁️ Visibility: Toggle the bell on/off globally

What Happens Next?

Once users subscribe through this bell:

  • Their subscription is stored in your PushSaaS database
  • They're linked to this specific site (Site ID: 1)
  • You can send them push notifications through PushSaaS campaigns
  • You can segment them and target specific audiences
  • You can track engagement metrics in your dashboard
